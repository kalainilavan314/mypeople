# My People logo

These are the master files of the logo, cleaned on 13 November 2026 from the 512 × 512 transparent logo (`app/public/logo.png` at the time). That logo had been cut out of a white background, so its outline kept a pale, white-mixed rim. The rim is now the logo's own colours fading to transparent, and the gaps between the figures are transparent.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `my-people-logo-transparent.png` | 512 × 512, transparent background: on screens, coloured backgrounds and photos |
| `my-people-logo-white.png` | 574 × 574, the same logo on white with a small margin: documents and places that need a solid background |

Every logo file in the apps is made from the transparent master, keeping that file's size and the logo's position:
- **Member app** (`apps/member-mobile/assets/images/`): `icon.png`, `adaptive-icon.png`, `splash-icon.png`, `favicon.png`.
- **Member PWA** (`app/public/`): `logo.png`, `favicon.png`, `icons/icon-192.png`, `icons/icon-512.png`, `icons/icon-512-maskable.png`.
- **Dashboard:** `apps/admin-web/src/app/icon.png`.

Opaque icons have a white background.

512 pixels is the largest version of the logo the project has. Print and large banners need the designer's original artwork (vector or a large PNG); ask for it rather than enlarging these files.
