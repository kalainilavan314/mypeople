# Assisted verification session

A designated reviewer, or a trained Tier 1 member, guides a member through identity capture when the member's device has no camera, the automated checks fail three times, or the member asks: a witness checklist, handing the device to the member, notes. The session cannot be submitted until the checklist is complete.

The verification queue and decisions are not on the staff app: they are the dashboard's W01 and W02 (`apps/admin-web/src/app/(staff)/verification`). This folder was `identity-reviews` and described both; it was narrowed on 27 October 2026 so the queue is built once.
