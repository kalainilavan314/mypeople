LOGIN HERO IMAGE
================

Drop your photo in THIS folder named exactly:

    login-hero.jpg

It becomes the full-width image at the top of the Login screen.

Tips:
- Portrait / tall crop works best (it's shown ~300px tall, full width).
- The important part of the photo should sit in the upper two-thirds — the
  bottom fades into the dark navy so the "Welcome!" text stays readable.
- JPG or PNG is fine; if you use PNG, either rename it to login-hero.jpg or
  change the filename in src/screens/auth/parts.tsx (AuthHero).
- Keep it reasonably small (< 400 KB) so it loads fast on budget phones.

Until you add the file, the login shows a tasteful navy + green gradient with
the Palmyra emblem, so nothing looks broken.
